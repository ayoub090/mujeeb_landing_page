# Mujeeb organic acquisition operating system

## North-star outcome

The commercial target is 1,000,000 SAR in annual recurring revenue, not “1,000K followers.” The working revenue mix is 20 Scale accounts at 5,999 SAR/month plus 30 Growth accounts at 2,499 SAR/month: 1,079,400 SAR ARR.

## One funnel

`Useful content → sector page → free 50-order pilot → measured result → paid plan → referral`

Every public link must use the same destination and campaign naming:

`https://usemujeeb.com/?utm_source={channel}&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=pilot_50_orders&utm_content={asset}#book`

Allowed sources: `reddit`, `medium`, `linkedin`, `x`, `github`, `meta`, `whatsapp`, `email`, `pinterest`.

## Ideal first customer

- Saudi or Moroccan ecommerce merchant with COD orders;
- at least 300 orders/month;
- manual confirmation or material failed-delivery cost;
- decision maker can provide a 50-order sample and baseline data;
- reachable with consent or through a relevant public business contact.

## Weekly capacity: 5–10 hours

### Monday — evidence (90 minutes)

- Interview two merchants or analyse two public complaints.
- Record the exact workflow, failed-delivery cost, objection, and language used.
- Update one sector page only when evidence changes the message.

### Tuesday — distribution (90 minutes)

- Publish one insight-led LinkedIn/X post.
- Answer two relevant Reddit questions with a complete answer; link only when it adds value and disclose affiliation.
- Repurpose the insight into one Pinterest visual or Meta post.

### Wednesday — outbound (120 minutes)

- Research 20 qualified merchants.
- Contact at most 10 with a personalised observation.
- Log source, consent basis, message, response, and opt-out status.

### Thursday — follow-up (60 minutes)

- Follow up only with prospects who consented, replied, or have a relevant published business contact.
- Stop immediately after an opt-out.

### Friday — conversion review (60 minutes)

- Review visits by UTM source, form starts, submitted leads, booked demos, pilots, and paid customers.
- Keep the channel with conversations and pilots; pause vanity traffic.

## Channel rules

### WhatsApp

Use one-to-one messages, not bulk blasts. Identify Mujeeb, state why the merchant is relevant, offer a 50-order pilot, and include a simple opt-out. No scraped personal numbers and no repeated messages without engagement.

### Email

Use public role-based business addresses or explicit opt-in. Send a maximum four-message sequence over ten days, each with identity, relevance, and unsubscribe instructions. Do not buy lists.

### Reddit

Lead with the answer. No link in the first interaction unless requested or indispensable. Disclose “I’m building Mujeeb” whenever the product is mentioned.

### Medium and GitHub

Publish original operational research, templates, and pilot methodology. Avoid copied articles and keyword-stuffed repository pages. GitHub points to the canonical site; the website remains the canonical source.

### Meta, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest

Use educational clips, checklists, and real pilot learnings. Paid campaigns begin only after at least three paying customers or a pilot-to-paid conversion rate supported by enough data.

## CRM stages

`researched → contacted → replied → qualified → demo_booked → pilot_started → pilot_completed → won/lost`

Required fields: business name, public URL, country, sector, estimated volume, current workflow, contact source, lawful basis/consent, UTM source, last contact, next action, opt-out, and outcome.

## Weekly scorecard

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Qualified accounts researched | 20 |
| Personalised first contacts | 10 |
| Useful public contributions | 3 |
| Positive conversations | 4 |
| Demos | 2 |
| Pilots started | 1 |
| Paid conversions | measured, no invented target |

The first optimization goal is one paid customer with a documented before/after result. Scale traffic only after the funnel proves it can produce that outcome.
