# Mujeeb | مجيب

[Mujeeb](https://usemujeeb.com/) is an Arabic AI order-confirmation service for ecommerce teams. It helps merchants confirm or cancel cash-on-delivery orders before fulfillment through the channel that fits the customer, while the merchant keeps its existing carrier and telecom choices.

## The problem

Unconfirmed orders consume packing time, shipping cost, and support capacity. Mujeeb gives the operations team a clear outcome before dispatch:

- confirmed;
- cancelled;
- needs human follow-up.

The pilot is intentionally small: a merchant tests Mujeeb on 50 real orders over seven days, compares it with the current manual process, and decides from measured results.

## Measure the pilot

- Reach rate
- Confirmation rate
- Cost per confirmed order
- Return-to-origin rate
- Hours of manual follow-up avoided
