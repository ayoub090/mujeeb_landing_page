Mujeeb Brand Guidelines

Brand Strategy & Overview الهوية والرؤية

Mujeeb (مجيب - Responder) is an Arabic AI-driven order confirmation service built for e-commerce stores in the GCC. By shifting COD confirmation from manual phone calls to conversational WhatsApp interfaces, Mujeeb eliminates shipping waste and streamlines post-checkout logic.

Our identity stands on four pillars: Empirical Integrity, Cultural Fluency, Respectful Boundaries, and Seamless Operations. We present a reliable, modern, and localized tool that honors the merchant’s systems and the customer's time.
