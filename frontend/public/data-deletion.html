Request data deletion

Mujeeb merchants can request deletion from the Privacy & Security area of their dashboard. You can also email support@usemujeeb.com from the address used for your Mujeeb account.

Include: your Mujeeb account email and Shopify store domain. Do not send passwords, access tokens, customer lists, or identity documents unless our support team provides a secure and necessary verification method.

We acknowledge verified requests, remove or anonymize eligible records, and complete the request within the applicable legal or Shopify deadline. Limited records may be retained only when required by law, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

Shopify customer requests should normally be submitted to the merchant first. Mujeeb processes Shopify's mandatory customer and shop redaction webhooks for connected stores.
