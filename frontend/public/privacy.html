Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Mujeeb is operated by Ayoub Fadil. This policy explains how Mujeeb processes information when a merchant visits our website, creates an account, connects a Shopify store, or uses order-confirmation services.

Data we process

Merchant account data can include name, business email, phone number, store name, plan, and integration status. For connected Shopify stores, Mujeeb processes only the order data required to provide the service: order identifiers, customer name and phone number, shipping address, order items, amount, currency, fulfilment status, and customer responses such as confirmation or a shared delivery location.

Why we process data

We use this information to import orders, contact customers on the merchant's instructions, confirm or cancel orders, verify delivery details, update the merchant dashboard, provide support, prevent abuse, and comply with legal or platform requirements. We do not sell personal data and do not use Shopify customer data for unrelated advertising.

Shopify and service providers

Mujeeb receives data through Shopify APIs after the merchant authorizes the app. We may use infrastructure, messaging, analytics, and payment providers only as necessary to deliver the service. These providers are limited by contract or platform terms and receive only the information needed for their role.

Security and access

Data is encrypted in transit. Customer names, phone numbers, shipping addresses, and integration credentials are encrypted at rest by the application. Access is limited to authorized operation and support purposes. Secrets are stored outside the source code.

Retention and deletion

We keep merchant and order data only while it is needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the platform. A merchant can request export or deletion from Mujeeb. We also process Shopify's mandatory customer data requests, customer redaction requests, and shop redaction requests. When Shopify instructs us to redact or delete protected data, we remove or anonymize the relevant records within the applicable Shopify deadline, subject only to mandatory legal retention.

Choices and rights

Merchants and eligible data subjects may request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion. Customers should normally contact the Shopify merchant first because that merchant controls the commercial relationship. We assist merchants with verified requests.

Contact: support@usemujeeb.com. Please include the store domain and enough information for us to verify the request without sending unnecessary personal data.
