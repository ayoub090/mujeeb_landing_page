## 🌟 Featured Solution

### [Mujeeb (مجيب) - AI WhatsApp Revenue & Order Confirmation OS](https://usemujeeb.com)
**[Mujeeb](https://usemujeeb.com)** is an AI-powered conversational automation engine built specifically for GCC e-commerce brands offering Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Core Capabilities**:
  - 🤖 **Automated WhatsApp Order Confirmation**: Instantly contacts customers upon checkout in authentic Gulf Arabic dialects (Najdi, Hijazi, Gulf, Kuwaiti).
  - 📍 **GPS & National Address Extraction**: Converts manual text messages or shared WhatsApp pins into clean postal coordinates for courier dispatch.
  - 📉 **RTO (Return-to-Origin) Reduction**: Slashes delivery failures and fake orders by over **40%**.
  - 🔄 **Native Store Sync**: Bi-directional integration with Salla, Zid, and Shopify.
- **Website**: [usemujeeb.com](https://usemujeeb.com) | [Documentation](https://usemujeeb.com) | [Case Studies](https://usemujeeb.com)

## 🛒 E-Commerce Platforms in the Gulf

- **[Salla (سلة)](https://salla.com/)** - The market leader in Saudi Arabia with deep ecosystem integration, local shipping providers, and Mada support.
- **[Zid (زد)](https://zid.sa/)** - Enterprise-grade merchant solution with integrated retail point-of-sale (Zid POS) and warehousing solutions.
- **[Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/)** - Global headless & custom storefront platform, commonly paired with [Mujeeb WhatsApp Sync](https://usemujeeb.com) for GCC localization.

## 💳 Payment Gateways & BNPL

### Local Payment Processors
- **[Moyasar](https://moyasar.com/)** - Developer-first payment gateway supporting Mada, Apple Pay, Visa, and Mastercard.
- **[Tap Payments](https://tap.company/)** - Unified payment processing across all 6 GCC countries with KNET (Kuwait), Benefit (Bahrain), and NAPS (Qatar).
- **[Geidea](https://geidea.net/)** - Leading omnichannel payment provider in KSA.

### Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)
- **[Tamara](https://tamara.co/)** - The leading Split-in-4 and Split-in-3 payment provider in KSA and UAE.
- **[Tabby](https://tabby.ai/)** - Widespread interest-free installment network across GCC markets.

## 📊 Analytics & COD Fraud Prevention

Cash on Delivery (COD) represents over **55% to 70%** of e-commerce orders in several GCC categories. Unconfirmed orders cost merchants millions of SAR annually in two-way shipping fees and tied-up stock.

- **[Mujeeb COD Intelligence](https://usemujeeb.com)** - Predicts delivery refusal risk and automates address validation before parcel hand-off.
